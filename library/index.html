    About this library

    Each poem sits at one moment in the history of the world, with its date and place. The text and numbering are published and permanent. A poem may be improved later; every change is dated and the old text is kept. Over time it will grow beyond poetry into a record of historical fact, with each poem gaining an article that cites its sources.

    Honest limit: these poems are creative work, not a history textbook. For sources, use the Research center.
